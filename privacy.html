Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Floki Bot respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used when you interact with our Discord bot and website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, Floki Bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, moderation events, and voice activity information.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide bot functionality, moderation, event logging, voice tracking, security, and server management.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate and maintain the bot.

4. Data Retention

Information may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the bot's features, security, and operational requirements. Retention may depend on server configuration.

5. Data Requests

Server owners may contact us to ask about available data access or deletion procedures.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when our services or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Floki Bot support team through our official support channels.
