Floki Bot - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features, Floki Bot may process Discord User IDs, Server IDs, usernames, server settings, moderation logs, and voice channel activity.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used for server management, logging, moderation, security, and maintaining bot functionality.

3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored to maintain the bot's features. Data is retained only as reasonably necessary.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and services necessary to operate the bot.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information.

6. Data Deletion

Server owners may contact the development team to request deletion of stored server-related information.

7. Third-Party Services

Floki Bot operates on Discord and is subject to Discord's policies.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when necessary.

9. Contact

Contact the Floki Bot development team through its official support server.
